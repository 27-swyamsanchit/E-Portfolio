02 — M&A Modeling

Ambuja Cements / India Cements

A full acquisition model: standalone and pro-forma three-statement builds, Sources & Uses, purchase price allocation, and accretion/dilution.

Same-industry consolidation, South Indian cement capacity

HYPOTHETICAL DEAL · ACADEMIC MODEL · CLOSE ASSUMED 30-SEP-2026

Ambuja Cements acquires The India Cements Limited at a 25% takeover premium, split 50% cash / 50% stock, funded partly by ₹7,500 Cr of new three-tranche acquisition debt. The model quantifies goodwill from purchase price allocation and walks the deal through to pro-forma EPS and cash-flow-per-share accretion/dilution across a five-year forecast.

The result: dilutive to reported EPS throughout the explicit period, but modestly accretive to cash flow per share every year — a distinction the model traces to the non-cash D&A shield created by the PP&E step-up, and flags candidly alongside an unreconciled balance-sheet variance still open in this version.
