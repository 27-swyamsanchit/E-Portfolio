## 3. Credit Risk Analysis

### Power Grid & Reliance Industries

### Objective

To assess whether a company can generate sufficient cash flow to meet its debt-servicing obligations.

### Approach

Analysed:

* Debt / Equity
* Debt / EBITDA
* Net Debt / EBITDA
* Interest Coverage Ratio
* DSCR
* Current Ratio / Liquidity
* Operating Cash Flow
* Free Cash Flow
* Forecast Cash Flows

The model also linked **forecast cash flows with leverage and debt-service metrics** and examined the impact of additional borrowing under different growth scenarios.

### Key Question

> **Can the company generate sufficient cash to service and repay its debt?**

### Key Skills

`Credit Risk` `DSCR` `Debt Analysis` `Cash Flow Analysis` `Financial Modelling` `Scenario Analysis`
